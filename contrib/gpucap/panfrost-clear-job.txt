What this tells us
------------------

* The header is exactly what gpufs builds: Is64b=1, Type=Fragment (9),
  Index=1, Next=0, dependencies 0.  `Exception Status` is the GPU's
  writeback of DONE after the job ran.
* The chain is a **single fragment job on JS0 with Next=0**.  There is no
  tiler job for a clear: the clear is done by the framebuffer descriptor's
  per-render-target `Clear` colour (0xFF0000FF here) and `Write Enable`,
  and the fragment job just walks the tiles.
* The job payload's bounds are in **tiles**, not pixels: 0..3 for a 64x64
  image (16x16 tiles), while the FBD's Width/Height/Bound fields are in
  pixels (0..63).
* `Framebuffer` is the FBD address with low flags (`0xa009003`), i.e. the
  same `| 3` shape as gpufs' page-table entries, not a plain pointer.
* The FBD is 0xc0 bytes of parameters plus the 16-byte colour render target
  at +0xc0, then the CRC extension.  The fragment job sits 0x140 bytes
  after the FBD in the same BO.

Consequence for gpufs: the first GPU clear does not need a tiler job or a
shader.  It needs (a) a fully populated FBD, (b) a fragment job whose
payload points at it, and (c) both mapped into the address space.  That is
a much smaller step than the shader chain, and it writes straight into the
VOP scanout buffer.
